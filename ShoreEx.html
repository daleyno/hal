    Your day

    From Auke Bay to the ice

    A small boat, a naturalist and the humpbacks of Stephens Passage. Then a gravel trail to the foot of Mendenhall Glacier.

    Your guide reads the water for blows and bubble nets, and the boat slows to let the whales set the pace. Orcas, sea lions and bald eagles often join the scene.

    Ashore, about 1.5 miles of uneven gravel trail, with a few short inclines, leads to Nugget Falls. You'll be close enough to feel the cold air coming off the ice.
