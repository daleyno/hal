    About

    The steakhouse at sea

    Prime steaks, seafood and a deep wine list, served at lunch and dinner on white linen with the sea outside the window.

    Course follows course at an easy pace. USDA Prime steaks are aged 28 days and cut to order, and the sommelier can pair a glass with each one.

    Behind the scenes, the chefs bake bread and churn desserts each afternoon, so the menu changes a little every day.
